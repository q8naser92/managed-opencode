## Remaining acceptance work

- Complete native SAML browser/loopback sign-in and folder-picker/installer-wizard interaction; remaining native offline and real elapsed-timeout cases.
- Full backup/restore, recovery on a new machine, load/concurrency, long-running operation and organization-specific allowlisting.
- Entra application registration, actual bank gateway TLS/model behavior, real SMTP and chosen third-party connector OAuth compatibility.
- Authenticode signing, independent clean source rebuild, complete dependency/license audit and production operational approval.

There is no overall-product PASS. The package is an unsigned pilot candidate with known validation limits. Synthetic lab gateway responses, SMTP and connectors prove the tested protocol paths; they do not establish production-bank connectivity or a particular model’s quality.
